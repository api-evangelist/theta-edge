Soongsil University's HUMANE Lab joins Theta's academic network as the 36th member, leveraging EdgeCloud's GPU infrastructure for research on misinformation, bias, and fairness in large language models. The lab has achieved notable success at FEVER challenges, securing first place in 2026 and back-to-back second-place finishes in prior years.
