We are pleased to announce that Dr. Ferheen Ayaz, Lecturer in Computer Science at City St George’s, University of London and member of its Centre for Software Reliability, is working with Theta EdgeCloud to support her research into the security of edge AI systems. City St George’s was formed through the recent merger of City, University of London and St George’s, University of London, making it one of the U.K.’s largest and most research-active universities.
